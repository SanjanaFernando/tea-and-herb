**PROJECT BRIEF & REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL (RFP)**

Project Title: Enterprise Website Redesign, AI Integration & Digital Ecosystem  
Client: THE TEA & HERB COMPANY LTD — A Premier Sri Lankan Tea Export House

1\. Executive Summary & Company Profile

We are an established, vertically integrated Ceylon Tea export house approaching 30 years of corporate heritage, backed by a leadership and master tea-tasting team with 40+ years of industry expertise.  
Unlike fragmented exporters, we are 100% self-sufficient under one roof. From bush to cup, our state-of-the-art production facility houses tea tasting, blending, flavoring, value-added packaging, warehousing, quality assurance, and administration in a single location. Strategically positioned in close proximity to the Port of Colombo and Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA), we offer unmatched logistics speed and supply chain reliability.

2\. Project Objective

Our current website retains an attractive heritage feel, but its underlying technology is outdated, non-responsive on modern devices, and unable to support our multi-channel global operations.  
We are seeking an expert web design and AI engineering partner to build a completely new, modern, and visually stunning digital flagship (or comprehensively re-engineer our existing foundation) that:  
• Elevates our brand to world-class international luxury and B2B industrial standards.  
• Serves every commercial avenue: Bulk Exports, A–Z Private Label & Brand Development, Own Retail Brands, International Distributor/Agent Recruitment, and Tea Tourism.  
• Integrates modern Artificial Intelligence (AI) for 24/7 global buyer engagement, lead qualification, and AI Search Visibility (GEO/AEO).  
• Allows us to adopt new, modern backend software (CMS, CRM, booking engine, and catalog management) cleanly and securely.

3\. Core Functional Pillars & Site Architecture

A. Multi-Channel Commercial Pathways (Smart Buyer Funneling)

The site must clearly guide different visitor personas without clutter:  
• Bulk & Commodity Exports: Interactive digital catalog of Ceylon tea grades (Orthodox, CTC, Green, White, Specialty/Single Estate) with downloadable technical spec sheets, harvest calendars, and sample request workflows.  
• A–Z Private Label & Product Development: A dedicated showcase of our turnkey OEM/private-label capabilities—highlighting custom blending, R\&D, tea bag/pouch/tin machinery, packaging design, and regulatory compliance support.  
• Own Brands & International Agents/Importers: A high-impact brand portfolio for our proprietary retail lines, paired with a B2B partner portal designed to recruit and onboard international distributors, importers, and regional agents.  
• Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) / Retail Readiness: Scalable architecture capable of supporting global retail e-commerce or regional store locators.

B. The "Under One Roof" & Strategic Logistics Showcase

• Bush-to-Cup Facility Tour: High-impact visual storytelling (video headers, interactive facility map, or 360° virtual walkthrough) demonstrating our in-house production, quality control labs, and warehousing.  
• Logistics Advantage: Dedicated section highlighting our proximity to Colombo Port and BIA, illustrating container turnaround times, air-freight agility, and global shipping reach.  
• Trust & Compliance Hub: Prominent, downloadable certifications (ISO, HACCP, FSSC 22000, GMP, Organic, Rainforest Alliance, Fairtrade, Ceylon Tea Lion Logo).

C. Interactive "Tea Tour" Experience & Booking Engine

• Experience Showcase: A rich, atmospheric section dedicated to our signature Tea Tour—detailing the itinerary, sensory tasting sessions with 40+ year veterans, factory walkthroughs, and heritage storytelling.  
• Seamless Booking: Integrated online reservation and inquiry system supporting individual tourists, corporate groups, and travel/tour operators, complete with calendar availability and automated confirmations.

4\. AI & Modern Software Requirements

We are open to adopting a modern, future-proof tech stack (e.g., Next.js / Headless CMS / modern enterprise CMS) and require the following AI capabilities:  
• 24/7 Multilingual AI Trade & Tea Concierge: A custom-trained AI assistant fluent in key export languages (English, Arabic, Russian, German, French, Mandarin, Japanese) trained on our tea grades, MOQs, private-label process, and Tea Tour details. It must qualify B2B leads and route them into a modern CRM.  
• AI Search & Answer Engine Optimization (GEO / AEO): Technical schema and content architecture engineered so global procurement managers asking AI platforms (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) for "trusted private label Ceylon tea manufacturers" or "Ceylon tea tours near Colombo" receive our company as a top recommendation.  
• Modern Software Stack: Recommendations for a clean, user-friendly Content Management System (CMS) for our internal team, plus seamless integration with a modern CRM (e.g., Zoho, HubSpot, or Odoo) to track export inquiries and tour bookings.

5\. Design, UX & Technical Standards

• Aesthetic Direction: Modern, super-attractive, and authoritative—blending rich Ceylon tea heritage with sleek, clean international corporate and luxury aesthetics.  
• Performance & Responsiveness: Mobile-first design with ultra-fast global load speeds (CDN-backed for fast viewing in Europe, the Middle East, North America, and Asia).  
• Security & Ownership: Enterprise-grade SSL/security, GDPR compliance, full source-code and IP ownership upon completion, and comprehensive staff training.

6\. Proposal Submission Guidelines

Interested agencies are requested to submit a proposal covering:  
1\. Recommended Approach: Your rationale for a clean-slate rebuild vs. re-engineering, including your proposed tech stack (CMS, AI framework, CRM/booking integrations).  
2\. Relevant Portfolio: Examples of B2B export, manufacturing, luxury hospitality/tourism, or AI-integrated websites you have delivered.  
3\. Project Roadmap & Timeline: Phased delivery schedule from UI/UX wireframing to AI training, testing, and launch.  
4\. Commercial Investment: Itemized cost breakdown (Design & Development, AI Integration, Content/Copywriting, and Annual Maintenance/Hosting).
